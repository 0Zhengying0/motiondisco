Improve walking step length for better task completion
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
The current program uses a step length of 0.30 meters, which results in a task completion error of 0.139 meters. By increasing the step length to 0.40 meters, we can reduce the number of walking steps required and potentially improve the task completion accuracy. This change is inspired by the system properties, which suggest that a step length of about 0.4 meters is optimal for walking steps.
</DESCRIPTION>
