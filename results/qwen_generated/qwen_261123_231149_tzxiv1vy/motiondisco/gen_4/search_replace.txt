Increase step length for walking
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
The current program uses a step length of 0.30 meters, which may be too short for the robot to cover the required distance efficiently. Increasing the step length to 0.40 meters, as suggested in the robot figures, should allow the robot to take fewer steps and reach the target more quickly. This change should improve the overall performance and reduce the number of modes, which could lead to a higher combined score.
</DESCRIPTION>
