Ensure proper transitions for the white box contact to avoid teleportation.

<DESCRIPTION>
The current plan has issues with the white box contact transitions, causing teleportation errors. To resolve this, we need to ensure that the white box transitions through a free state before changing its contact surface. This will prevent the solver from interpreting the transition as teleportation.

We will modify the `pick_up_object` and `place_object` functions to include a free state transition for the white box before and after the contact change.
