<DESCRIPTION>
The current plan has only 1 walking step, which may not be sufficient for the robot to move to the desired position. By increasing the number of walking steps, we can give the solver more room to move and potentially reduce the trajectory optimization cost. This change is inspired by the system properties that suggest at least ceil(distance / 0.4) + 1 steps are needed for a move, and that a 2 m trip needs walk(plan, 6) steps.
