Fix contact transition issue by adding intermediate free modes.
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
The current plan has contact transitions that violate the solver's rule: a contact must be released before it touches a different surface. Specifically, the left_ee and right_ee are transitioning directly from the table to the box without an intermediate free mode. This causes the solver to reject the plan.

To fix this, we need to add intermediate free modes before the robot transitions from the table to the box and from the box to the table. This ensures that the contacts are released before they touch a different surface, satisfying the solver's rule.
</DESCRIPTION>
