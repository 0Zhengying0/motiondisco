<DESCRIPTION>
This optimized version of the contact plan generator addresses the task of placing a box on the other side of a table. It ensures that the robot can reach, lift, move, and place the box while maintaining stability and minimizing violations. The plan is structured to include a sequence of walking steps, grasping, lifting, moving, placing, and final adjustments. Additionally, it includes corrections to ensure that the final mode matches the discrete contact goal.

The key improvements include:
1. Adding more walking steps to ensure the robot can reach the table and the box.
2. Correctly releasing the box from the floor before attempting to move it.
3. Ensuring that the robot's hands and feet maintain proper contact types during transitions.
4. Correcting the final mode to match the required contact goal.

This approach aims to provide a more feasible and efficient plan that scores higher on the combined performance metrics.
