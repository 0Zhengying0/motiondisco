A simple yet effective approach to generate a contact plan for grasping a banana. The plan starts from the initial mode and appends modes to move the robot towards the banana, ensuring that the left and right hands make contact with the banana's respective sides.

<DESCRIPTION>
The approach is straightforward: start from the initial mode where the robot's feet are on the floor and the hands are free. Append modes to move the robot towards the banana, ensuring that the left and right hands make contact with the banana's respective sides. The plan is extended by appending modes that move the robot's base closer to the banana and then making contact with the banana's left and right sides. This approach ensures that the final mode matches the discrete contact goal.
</DESCRIPTION>

from __future__ import annotations

from typing import Any, Dict, List, Tuple

def generate_contact_plan(scene: List[Dict[str, Any]]) -> Dict[str, List[Tuple]]:
    # Start from the initial mode
    plan = {ch: list(seq) for ch, seq in get_initial_mode().items()}
    
    # Append modes to move the robot towards the banana
    # Move the robot's base closer to the banana
    walk(plan, 5)
    
    # Append modes to make contact with the banana's left and right sides
    append_mode(plan, left_ee=(9, 1), right_ee=())
    append_mode(plan, left_ee=(9, 1), right_ee=(11, 1))
    
    return plan

from env import append_mode, get_default_scene, get_initial_mode, walk

def run_planning():
