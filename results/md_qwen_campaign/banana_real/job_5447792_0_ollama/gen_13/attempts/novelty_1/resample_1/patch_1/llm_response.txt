<DESCRIPTION>
To improve the contact planning algorithm, I drew inspiration from the provided context programs and introduced a novel approach that focuses on ensuring feasibility and goal satisfaction. The key insights were:

1. **Initial Setup**: Start with the initial mode and ensure that the robot's base is stable by grounding both feet on the floor.
2. **Feasibility**: Use the `walk` function to ensure the robot can reach the banana by appending walking steps to the plan.
3. **Grasping**: Append a mode where the robot's hands make contact with the banana.
4. **Goal Matching**: Ensure the final mode matches the discrete contact goal by setting the left and right end-effectors to contact the specified banana faces.

The algorithm ensures that the plan is feasible by providing enough walking steps to reach the banana and by maintaining stable support on the ground. This approach avoids the issues encountered in previous plans, such as the robot's feet being too far from the floor and slipping contacts.

    append_mode(plan, **channel_updates) -> None
