A novel approach that optimizes the sequence of contact modes by explicitly managing the transitions between different contact states. This approach ensures that all contact changes are handled correctly, avoiding the common pitfalls of abrupt transitions that lead to solver infeasibility. The key insights are:

1. **Explicit Transition Management**: Each contact transition is carefully planned to ensure that the robot's limbs and objects move smoothly from one contact state to another. This includes releasing contacts before making new ones and ensuring that objects are lifted and placed correctly.

2. **Smooth Motion Planning**: The plan includes intermediate modes to ensure that the robot's movements are smooth and feasible. This involves walking steps to move the robot's base and adjusting the object's position gradually.

3. **Goal-Oriented Planning**: The plan is structured to achieve the task goal by breaking it down into a series of sub-tasks, such as moving to the object, grasping it, lifting it, and placing it on the table.

4. **Constraint Handling**: The plan adheres to the constraints of the environment, such as the robot's reach and the object's size, ensuring that all contacts are within feasible limits.

    append_mode(plan, **channel_updates) -> None
