An improved approach to generating a contact mode sequence that addresses the limitations of previous implementations by ensuring proper contact transitions and maintaining feasibility.

<DESCRIPTION>
The previous implementations encountered issues with contact transitions, where objects changed surfaces without being properly released first. This led to solver errors indicating invalid contact schedules. The new approach focuses on ensuring that each contact transition is valid by introducing intermediate modes where necessary. Additionally, it optimizes the walking steps to ensure the robot reaches the target efficiently while maintaining feasibility.

Key insights:
1. **Contact Transition Validity**: Each object must pass through a free state () before touching a different surface.
2. **Feasibility in Walking**: The walking steps should be sufficient to reach the target without causing solver errors.
3. **Contact Types**: Using the correct contact types (sticking vs. sliding) to maintain a stable grasp.

The new approach breaks down the task into clear steps, ensuring each transition is valid and the plan is feasible.
