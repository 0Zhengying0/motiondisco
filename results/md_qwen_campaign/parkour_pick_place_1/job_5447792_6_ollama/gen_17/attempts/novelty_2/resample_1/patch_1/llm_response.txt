A novel approach that improves the planning by integrating a more sophisticated decision-making process for moving the robot and manipulating objects. This approach involves:

1. **Initial Setup**: Start with the initial mode and ensure the robot is in a stable configuration.
2. **Move to Box**: Use the `walk` function to move the robot closer to the box, ensuring the base is positioned correctly.
3. **Grasp the Box**: Use the left hand to grasp the box, then lift it using the `append_mode` function.
4. **Move to Table**: Continue walking to reach the table.
5. **Place the Box**: Place the box on the table using the left hand, ensuring the final mode matches the discrete contact goal.

The key insight is to break down the task into clear, sequential steps and ensure that each step is feasible before moving to the next.
