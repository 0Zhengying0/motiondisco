<DESCRIPTION>
The previous attempts resulted in infeasible contact schedules due to improper transitions, particularly with the object. This edit proposes a more structured approach to ensure the robot transitions properly between modes, especially for object manipulation. Specifically, it ensures the object is released before changing its contact, and that the end-effector is free before changing its contact.
